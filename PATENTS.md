GOVERNED OBJECTS SPECIFICATION PATENT NON-ASSERTION COMMITMENT

1. Commitment. Brafford Labs, LLC, for itself and its Affiliates
("we"), irrevocably promises not to assert any Necessary Claim against
any person or entity for making, having made, using, selling, offering
for sale, importing or distributing an Implementation of a Final
Specification, subject to Section 4.

2. Definitions.
   "Final Specification" means a version of the Governed Objects
   Specification that we have published and labeled Final at https://github.com/governedobjects.
   Drafts are not covered.
   "Implementation" means software, hardware or a service, to the extent
   it implements the normative requirements (required or optional) of a
   Final Specification.
   "Necessary Claims" means patent claims, owned or controlled by us now
   or in the future, that would necessarily be infringed by implementing
   those normative requirements, because no commercially reasonable
   non-infringing way to implement them exists. Claims covering
   technologies the Specification uses but does not define (for example
   operating systems, databases, networking or cryptographic primitives)
   are not Necessary Claims.
   "Affiliate" means an entity that controls, is controlled by, or is
   under common control with another entity.

3. Duration. This commitment is irrevocable for each Final Specification
   once published, except as stated in Section 4. Withdrawing or
   changing this document affects only Final Specifications published
   afterward.

4. Defensive termination. If you or your Affiliate start a lawsuit or
   other proceeding asserting that any Implementation of the Governed
   Objects Specification infringes a patent, this commitment ends for
   you and your Affiliates as of that date. A counterclaim in a
   proceeding that someone else first brought against you does not end
   it.

5. Successors. This commitment binds anyone to whom we transfer a
   Necessary Claim, and we will make any transfer subject to it.

6. No other rights. This commitment grants no rights under any other
   patents, trademarks or intellectual property, and does not modify
   the Apache License 2.0 under which the Specification text is
   licensed.

7. Contributors. Anyone who contributes normative text to the
   Specification makes this same commitment for that contribution, as
   described in GOVERNANCE.md.
