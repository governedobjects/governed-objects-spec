GOVERNED OBJECTS TRADEMARK POLICY

Our mark. "Governed Objects"(TM) is a trademark of
Brafford Labs, LLC. The specification is openly licensed; this
name is not. This policy explains how to refer to them fairly.

Ordinary language. This policy does not restrict use of words in their
ordinary descriptive meaning.

You may, without asking:
- State truthfully that your product "implements the Governed Objects
  Specification, v0alpha1 draft2.2".
- Write articles, tutorials, books, talks and courses about Governed
  Objects, and link to our materials.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to our specification or products.

You may not, without written permission:
- Use our marks, or anything confusingly similar, in a product, company,
  service, domain or package name.
- Use our logos, or suggest that we endorse, certify or are affiliated
  with your product.
- Call a modified version of the specification the "Governed Objects
  Specification" or a similar name. The Apache License lets you modify
  and publish it, but you must give it a different name and state that
  it is derived from, and is not, the Governed Objects Specification.

Conformance claims. You do not need our permission to make a truthful
conformance claim.
- "Implements" means you have implemented the specification in good
  faith.
- "Conforms to" or "compatible with" the Governed Objects Specification
  version and revision (for example v0alpha1 draft2.2) requires passing
  the openly available conformance suite for that revision and naming
  the suite version used.
- Passing the suite is necessary but not sufficient to claim
  conformance. You must also satisfy `CONF-CLAIM-001` in the
  specification: identify the version and revision exactly, claim Core,
  identify each optional profile, role, and conditional capability you
  claim, satisfy every normative requirement those claims apply to, and
  disclose documented deviations and unresolved encoding assumptions.
- A conformance claim must name both the specification version/revision
  and the conformance suite version used, for example "conforms to the
  Governed Objects Specification v0alpha1 draft2.2 (conformance suite
  0.1.0)". You must keep the suite results that support the claim. If
  we ask in writing, you must provide them within 30 days. You may
  provide them confidentially. If you do not provide supporting results
  within that time, the claim is treated as unsupported, and you must
  correct or withdraw it.

Certification (optional). "Certified", "official" and the certification
mark are reserved for products under a signed certification agreement
with us. Certification is an optional extra. It is never required to
claim conformance. If a neutral foundation or similar body is formed for
the specification, we will transfer the certification mark and program
to it. No certification program exists yet.
